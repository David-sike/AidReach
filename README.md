# AIDReach

AIDReach is a platform focused on making aid and support services easier to find, understand, and access.

## Purpose

Finding the right help can be difficult, especially when information is scattered or unclear. AIDReach aims to bring useful resources into one accessible place and help people connect with the support they need.

## Planned capabilities

- Discover relevant aid and support resources
- Search and filter services by need, location, or eligibility
- Present clear, up-to-date resource information
- Help organizations make their services easier to reach
